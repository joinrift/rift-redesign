Questions, answered.

The core actives in every Rift Hair formula — minoxidil and finasteride — are FDA-approved medications for hair loss, with decades of clinical use behind them. Your combination is compounded per prescription in a U.S. licensed pharmacy; like all compounded medicine, the combined product itself isn’t separately FDA-approved, which is exactly why a licensed provider reviews every prescription.

The actives in Rift Hair — finasteride and minoxidil — are the two most studied hair loss treatments in existence. In long-term clinical studies, 97% of men on finasteride stopped losing hair and 90% saw improved coverage. No treatment takes you from fully bald to a full head of hair, but for thinning hair, this is the strongest evidence in medicine.

Most men notice reduced shedding in the first 6–8 weeks and visible regrowth around months 3–6, with best results between months 4–9. Take progress photos monthly — the change sneaks up on you.

These are real medications, and a small percentage of men experience side effects — in studies, roughly 0.3% discontinue finasteride for that reason. Your provider reviews your history before prescribing, and the topical option (Regrow) exists precisely for men who want minimal systemic exposure. You can message your care team anytime.

Orals (Fortify, Apex) are the easiest to stay consistent with — one tablet, done. Topical (Regrow) targets the scalp directly with minimal systemic exposure. Share your preference in the intake; your provider makes the final call.
